Privacy Policy

GPS Camera by Gatha Media
Effective date: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GPS Camera (“the App”), published under the Gatha Media brand, accesses and uses camera and location information. The App is designed to create photographs with a location stamp.

1. Information the App accesses

  • Camera: The App accesses the camera when you use it to take a photograph.
  • Device location: If you grant location permission, the App obtains location information such as latitude and longitude to create the GPS stamp. The accuracy available depends on your device, settings, environment and the permission you grant.
  • Address information: The App uses Android’s Geocoder interface to try to convert coordinates into a readable address. Address availability and accuracy depend on the Android device and its geocoding provider.
  • Photographs: The App creates and saves the stamped photograph on your device. The App is not designed to upload your photographs to Gatha Media servers.

2. How information is used

Camera and location access are used to capture a photograph and add the location details you choose to include in its stamp. The App does not require an account to use these core features.

3. Storage and sharing

Based on the App’s intended operation, stamped photographs are saved locally on your device and the App does not intentionally send photographs or location records to Gatha Media servers. The App does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, according to the current developer-provided project information.

Address lookup and system services: Android’s Geocoder may rely on a device manufacturer’s or system provider’s service, which can process coordinates to return an address and may require internet access. That processing is governed by the relevant Android/device provider’s practices, not by Gatha Media. The exact provider can vary by device. If you do not want to use address lookup, you can deny or revoke location permission; some GPS-stamp features may then be unavailable or incomplete.

4. Permissions and your choices

You can grant or deny camera and location permissions through Android’s permission prompts and Settings. You can revoke permissions at any time. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working. You control the photographs stored in your device’s gallery and file storage.

5. Third-party services

The App does not intentionally send data to Gatha Media-operated servers. Android operating-system services and the geocoding provider used by your device may process information as described above. Please review the applicable privacy information provided by your device or system-service provider for details of their handling of data.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children. The App does not ask users to create an account or submit personal information to Gatha Media.

7. Security and retention

Stamped photographs are stored on your device. Their security and retention depend in part on your device’s security settings, storage configuration, backups and any other apps with which you choose to share them. You can delete photographs using your device’s gallery or file manager.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
