Privacy Policy

GPS Camera Pro (Research Edition) by Gatha Media
Effective date: 9 November 2026

Zero Data Collection Policy: GPS Camera Pro is engineered for institutional field research. We do not collect, transmit, upload, or monetize your data. All captures, audio notes, and telemetry logs are stored 100% locally on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how GPS Camera Pro (“the App”), published under the Gatha Media brand, accesses and uses your device's camera, microphone, location, and hardware sensors. The App is a spatial documentation instrument designed to create location-stamped photographs, qualitative audio notes, and quantitative mapping logs.

1. Information the App accesses

  • Camera: The App accesses the camera to capture high-resolution photographs and provide viewfinder capabilities (like the 3x3 grid and dual-axis leveler).
  • Microphone: If you utilize the "Audio Field Notes" feature, the App accesses your microphone to record synchronized spoken observations.
  • Device Location & Sensors: If granted location permission, the App pulls coordinates (latitude/longitude/altitude) directly from GPS satellites (bypassing the device clock for anti-spoofing). It also accesses hardware sensors (accelerometer and magnetometer) to record pitch, roll, and compass azimuth bearing.
  • Address information: The App uses Android’s Geocoder interface to attempt to convert your coordinates into a readable address (reverse-geocoding).

2. How information is used

Camera, microphone, and location/sensor access are used strictly to compile your field documentation. The App processes this data to stamp visual cards onto your photographs, inject deep EXIF metadata (like custom project notes and compass bearings) into the image files, and generate automated CSV and KML mapping logs. The App requires no account, no login, and works entirely offline (excluding the Android Geocoder).

3. Storage and sharing

100% Local Storage: All original photographs, stamped photographs, audio memos (.m4a), and telemetry logs (CSV/KML files) are generated and saved strictly to your local device storage (e.g., Pictures/GPS Camera/ and Downloads/GPS Camera Logs/). The App is intentionally built without internet upload capabilities, advertising SDKs, or cloud analytics.

Address lookup and system services: Android’s Geocoder relies on a device manufacturer’s or system provider’s service (like Google Play Services) to process coordinates into a readable address, which may require brief internet access. That processing is governed by your device provider’s practices, not by Gatha Media. If you do not want to use address lookup, you can disable the feature in the App's settings or run the device offline; your coordinates and metadata will still save accurately.

4. Permissions and your choices

You have absolute control over your permissions. You can grant or deny Camera, Microphone, and Location permissions through Android’s system settings at any time. Denying a specific permission will simply disable the corresponding feature (e.g., denying the microphone will disable Audio Field Notes, but the camera will still function). You retain complete ownership and control over all files generated by the App in your device's file manager.

5. Third-party services

The App contains no third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising frameworks. Android operating-system services (such as the Geocoder or FusedLocationProvider) may process information locally to provide the App with location data. Please review the applicable privacy information provided by your device manufacturer or operating system for details on their handling of hardware requests.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is a professional research tool and is not directed at children. The App does not ask users to create an account or submit personal information to Gatha Media.

7. Security and retention

Because all data remains on your device, security and retention are managed by you. The security of your field research depends on your device’s lock screen, encryption settings, backups, and any file-sharing apps you actively choose to use (such as sharing an image to email or importing a KML file to QGIS). You can delete your photographs, audio notes, and logs using the App's native gallery or your device’s file manager at any time.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App’s features evolve (such as adding new local data export formats). The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
